# Dashboard-Using-Microsoft-Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Supply Chain Performance Dashboard** is an interactive Microsoft Excel project designed to analyze supply chain operations and provide actionable business insights through dynamic dashboards. The project transforms raw supply chain data into meaningful visual reports, enabling stakeholders to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), evaluate operational efficiency, and support data-driven decision-making.

This project demonstrates the complete data analysis workflow, including data cleaning, data preparation, KPI development, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, interactive slicers, and business storytelling.

## 🎯 Business Objective

The dashboard was developed to help management answer three critical business questions:

- **What happened?** – Executive overview of business performance.
- **Why did it happen?** – Product-level revenue and profitability analysis.
- **What action should be taken?** – Supplier, logistics, and operational performance analysis.

## 📈 Dashboard 1 – Executive Summary
Provides a high-level overview of business performance through key metrics and product-wise analysis.

**KPIs**

- Average Defect Rate

**Visualizations**
- Revenue by Product Category
- Profit by Product Category

### 📊 Dashboard 2 – Product Performance
Analyzes product profitability and revenue contribution.

**KPIs**

- Top Profit Product Category

**Visualizations**

- Top 10 Profitable Products
- Profit Contribution by Product Category

### 🚚 Dashboard 3 – Operations & Supply Chain

**KPIs**

**Visualizations**
